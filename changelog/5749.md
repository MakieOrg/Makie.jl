## ComputePipeline

- Added `select!(graph, selector, choices, output)` as a way to resolve just one of `choices` based on the index given by `selector` and write it to `output`. This allows you to exclude expensive branches of the compute graph with an adjustable index. Alternatively, `select!(graph, condition, true_choice, false_choice, output)` can be used to make the selection based on a boolean node. [#5749](https://github.com/MakieOrg/Makie.jl/pull/5749)
