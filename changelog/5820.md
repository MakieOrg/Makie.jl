- `annotation` places labels with a new candidate-based algorithm that avoids overlaps, covered points and crossing leaders.
- `annotation` keeps labels with given offsets or positions fixed while placing the remaining ones around them.
- `annotation` no longer draws leaders for labels that sit within the `shrink` distance of their point.
- `annotation` leaders with `Ann.Paths.Line` attach to a pill shape around the label instead of pointing at its center, `Ann.Paths.Line(attach = :center)` keeps the old behavior.
- `annotation` uses the ink bounding box of each label for placement and leader clipping.
