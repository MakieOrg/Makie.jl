## Breaking

- WGLMakie now requires Bonito 5.3 and Julia 1.11 or later for session ownership and browser-listener cleanup.

## Changed

- Reduced WGLMakie first-display compilation and Axis construction work, and added an optional full precompile workload.
- Switched WGLMakie browser tests to ElectronCall and enabled rendering on demand by default.

## Fixed

- Figures mounted independently can recover missing glyphs without waiting for an earlier figure to mount.
- Inserting or deleting plots while disconnected no longer blocks; the browser receives a fresh scene snapshot when it reconnects.
