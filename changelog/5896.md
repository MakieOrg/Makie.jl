## Breaking

- `is_mouseinside(scene)` now also requires all ancestors of `scene` to be visible and the mouse to be inside their viewports, and returns `false` while another scene captures the mouse (`Scene.captures_mouse`).
- Blocks placed in a `Scene` use window coordinates for their bbox, also inside scenes with an offset viewport.

## Added

- Added `Scene.captures_mouse` to let a scene claim the mouse for itself, its ancestors and its descendants (e.g. a modal overlay).

## Fixed

- Scenes are now clipped to the viewports of all their ancestors and hidden together with them in all backends.
